## Trailer music

`public/assets/trailer_music.mp3` is an original procedural cue generated by [scripts/generate-trailer-music.py](scripts/generate-trailer-music.py). It uses synthesized oscillators only, with no recordings or external samples, and is dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The [dedication notice](licenses/trailer-music-CC0.txt) covers the cue and its copied demo soundtrack; no attribution is required. The generator source remains under MIT.

Regenerate it with `python3 scripts/generate-trailer-music.py` (Python 3 and ffmpeg with libmp3lame required). The prior soundtrack was replaced because its source and redistribution license could not be established from the repository. Unused duplicate music and font files were removed from `app/assets/`.
