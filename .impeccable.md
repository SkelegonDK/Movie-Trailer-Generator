## Design Context

### Users
People using the app as a creative tool or for marketing. They want to turn movie ideas into scripts, posters, narration, and downloadable trailers with little friction.

### Brand Personality
Fun, playful, approachable. The interface should make experimentation enjoyable and keep the next action obvious.

### Aesthetic Direction
Refine the existing dark palette, colorful controls, and Frick display typography. The quality bar is a ship-ready MVP: fix rough edges and preserve the app's character.

### Design Principles
- Put ease of use first: clear labels, useful empty states, and visible progress.
- Keep the experience fun without letting decoration compete with the workflow.
- Align repeated controls and use the shared components and semantic tokens.
- Make keyboard navigation, contrast, and touch controls dependable.
- Keep polish focused; preserve the established visual direction.
